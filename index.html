The Lantern moon is broken. The dead do not rest.
Greenhollow Vale · test build

Desktop: WASD or arrow keys to move. Click an enemy to target it, or press F / Tab to attack the nearest. You auto-attack when close. Skills 1–4, potion Q, talk or enter E, bag C, talents T, mouse wheel to zoom.

Phone: drag anywhere on the left of the screen to move. Tap ⚔️ to attack the nearest enemy, and use the skill buttons around it.

Talk to Captain Edda (yellow !) to begin. Your progress saves in this browser.
